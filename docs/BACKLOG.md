# mult — improvement backlog

Consolidated from a five-track audit (correctness, architecture, security, tests/perf,
UX/docs/tooling) run on 2026-08-05 against commit `0224885`.

Each item has an ID, a severity, the evidence location, and the intended fix. Items are
grouped by category; execution order lives in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).

Severity: **C** critical · **H** high · **M** medium · **L** low.
Status: `todo` · `wip` · `done` · `dropped` (with reason).

---

## Outcome

**117 items audited, 117 closed.** Every row is `done`; none was dropped, and
none of the original findings turned out to be a false alarm on inspection —
though two were materially different from the audit's description once verified
(F20's two "duplicate" helpers were not the same function, and F1's "verify
first" claim was proved rather than assumed, with the evidence recorded in the
row). Delivered over thirteen slices; see [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for the order and
the reasoning behind it.

One item is **carried forward** rather than closed: **A14**, below, found while
fixing A13 and not part of the original audit. It is a separate upstream defect
with a different shape, and the note in its row says why fixing it needs its own
design rather than a line in a cleanup slice.

**What the audit missed**, and the tooling it asked for then found:

- **A13** — a one-row or one-column PTY pane panics `fnug-vt100` on ordinary
  output. Found by the `vt_response_detector` fuzz target (G3) on its **first
  run**, i.e. by an item whose whole justification was that nobody had fuzzed
  this code. Reachable from a small enough terminal window.
- **A14** — a column shrink that truncates a double-width character leaves the
  emulator in a state where the next printed character panics. Found while
  establishing A13's safe floor, at ordinary sizes (80 → 41 columns with CJK
  content on screen), not at the edges A13 lives at.
- **G15** — filed as six unit tests depending on ambient directory privacy, and
  that was true, but it was the visible third of the problem. Behind it,
  `ensure_private_dir` could not succeed on *any* path in that sandbox (`/` maps
  to `nobody`, so the ancestor walk rejected everything), and nine `mult-server`
  dispatch tests were failing on `openpty`, because a Nix sandbox has a
  `/dev/ptmx` symlink with no `devpts` behind it. All three were pre-existing and
  all three were invisible: `nix flake check` had been failing earlier, at
  compile time, so nothing downstream of that had ever run. Each failure only
  became visible once the one in front of it was fixed.
- **H1** — the MSRV claim turned out to be *true*. It was audited as "almost
  certainly rotted"; 1.88 built the whole workspace, so the claim stayed and
  gained a CI job that proves it.

Three findings were also revised on contact with the code, and the rows record
both the original claim and what was actually there: E10's light-theme default
(deliberately not added — `NO_COLOR` covers it), F17's "unused" protocol
variants (three of them back a live `ListSessions` the daemon implements), and
C12 (resolved by A3's instance token rather than by the per-session token the
audit proposed).
